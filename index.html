About

Strategy to execution — end to end

Nearly a decade of IT operations across real estate, nonprofit, and higher education — running the same play at growing scale: modernize, consolidate, automate. I migrated a 300-person nonprofit to Azure AD and SSO, led a datacenter migration that cut equipment footprint 66%, then brought the playbook to St. Francis College — where I authored the modernization strategy adopted by the President's Cabinet and spent the next year delivering it: 7+ legacy platforms retired, the college consolidated onto Microsoft 365, and a promotion from consultant to Network Manager within nine months.

I operate at director scope: I own the vendor portfolio and licensing decisions, lead incident response as the institution's liaison to our managed-detection-and-response SOC, initiated the recruiting that rebuilt our support team, handle confidential personnel matters with HR and Legal, and hold recurring operations-improvement sessions with the President's Office.

My differentiator is that I still build. Microsoft Entra ID, Cisco, Azure/VMware, PowerShell + Microsoft Graph — I set the strategy and can execute any layer of it personally. Teams trust leaders who can do the work; executives trust leaders who can explain it.
