About

One person, many IT hats

I joined St. Francis College as a consultant in April 2025 and became Network Manager in January 2026. My title says "network," but the job covers everything. In that time I moved the college's single sign-on onto Microsoft Entra ID, moved its phones to Microsoft Teams, and replaced its portal vendor with a SharePoint "Digital Campus." I built 118 email lists that maintain themselves, and I wrote and shipped an admin tool for my team.

Along the way I've been an identity engineer, M365 architect, network engineer, incident responder, endpoint manager, telephony lead, PowerShell/Graph developer, data analyst, policy writer, vendor manager and trainer. Sometimes all in one day.

What I care about most is how people feel after they talk to IT. The best feedback I've ever received wasn't about a migration. A colleague told me she used to dread contacting IT at every job, "and that is NEVER the case when I reach out to you."

Selected Work

Things I've built and shipped

SFC Account Manager

A Windows console I designed, wrote and shipped. It puts Active Directory, Entra ID, Exchange Online, Defender and Intune in one window for the IT team. It comes in three role-based editions, with signed installers, automatic updates and a test suite. I launched it in person at the IT staff meeting. "Dang oh my gosh that is amazing… All of this is helpful thank you so much." (Richard McCarthy, IT Technician)

OneLogin → Microsoft Entra ID

I planned the move, presented it to the college's C-suite executives and president, and carried it out. That meant rebuilding 74 applications, running the cutover over the New Year's holiday, and retiring OneLogin within days. It launched with a single apps front door, self-service password reset and MFA, all backed by user and service-desk guides.

Smart Audiences

118 email audiences that update themselves from the student information system: by major, minor, program, cohort, term and employee type. When a student changes majors, their email lists, SharePoint access and Teams membership follow overnight, with no ticket needed.

SFC Digital Campus

The President appointed me to lead IT's side of replacing the college's third-party portal. The result is mySFC, yourSFC and SupportSFC on SharePoint: 3 hubs and 26 sites, built with PowerShell and Microsoft Graph and handed off to 17 departments. It went live in July 2026.

RingCentral → Microsoft Teams Phone

I wrote the business case and led the migration: 199 numbers ported and E911 validated. On go-live day I walked every room myself. Afterwards I redesigned the auto attendants and call queues and added business texting.

Intune Modernization

I designed four device personas (assigned, shared room, classroom podium, loaner) using Autopilot, BitLocker, LAPS and Windows Hello for Business, and set a strict no-wipe migration rule. I ran the pilots, wrote the guides, and trained a technician to deliver it.

KACE Service Desk Redesign

I rebuilt the college's help desk from the ground up. Every ticket category now maps to exactly one support tier, under a simple rule: "escalation changes the tier, not the category." I simplified the ticket form, added queues with email-to-ticket and monitoring alerts, and built SQL reporting. Then I trained the team with a live demo and wrote the plan to run six service desks.

IT SOP Catalogue & Process Tree

I built a complete IT operations library: 64 standard operating procedures (654 pages) and a 110-item forms pack of request forms, checklists, reference cards and published texts. It also includes a branded poster series, briefing materials, an explainer video, and an interactive Process Tree with a guided "let's help a user" triage path.

"The Dream"

A two-phase plan I presented to the President. Phase one is a tiered IT structure with a real career ladder: "Nobody manages work they have never done." Phase two meets every employee need with tools the college already licenses: "use, not purchase." It came with a department workshop and a 9-minute narrated film.

Teams Call Analytics

A Microsoft Graph collector that keeps call records past their built-in retention window. The analysis showed that a department's published number was mostly automated calls, while its real queue was on another line. That changed how leadership read its phone numbers.

One Security Stack

I planned and delivered the move of the college's separate antivirus and email-security vendors onto Microsoft Defender, covering Windows, macOS, Linux and servers. The result is one console, and both legacy products are fully retired.

Club Collaboration Spaces

Each student club gets its own Microsoft 365 Group/Team instead of a shared account whose password gets passed around. Every club has its own email, calendar, files and a roster the advisor manages. I scripted the setup; the first club had 148 students.

AI, Responsibly

I reviewed the Copilot and Claude terms with Legal and the CIO and designed a FERPA-conscious Copilot pilot for the Registrar, with a human in the loop. I also guide campus offices on using AI tools with student data.
