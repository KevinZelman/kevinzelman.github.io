  • ACC-15 · Acceptable Use & Credential-Sharing StandardWhat this standard adds to the organization’s existing acceptable use policy — and how IT applies it when something goes wrong.
  • ACC-16 · Application Onboarding, SSO & SAML Claims StandardWhat IT needs before an application is federated, which protocol it gets, the claim set every application receives — and how an application moves from one identity provider to another without breaking on the day.
  • ACC-17 · Periodic Access Review & Entitlement RecertificationWithout recertification, every grant becomes permanent by default. What is reviewed, on what cycle, by whom — and what actually happens when nobody answers.
